## Direction contract
THESIS: wc-Go as a soroban: counts are beads on rods, a chunk seam is a rod boundary, and the carry is the bead that moves up a column. Refuses input-left, results-right.
OWN-WORLD: black lacquer #120e0c ground; brass frame, vermilion and bone beads, gold rule; four rod groups for lines, words, bytes, chars; Shippori Mincho display, Zen Kaku Gothic New text, Noto Sans Mono for hex; mixed case.
STORY: The visitor sets chunk size on the frame, streams text, watches beads click up per count; at a seam a bead is carried; the reference row matches and the frame rests.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-bleed soroban: the frame with four labeled columns, the feed tray above, the carry bead marked, the reference row below; streaming text and presets beneath.
FORM: Soroban abacus, first on my list, chosen as IMPECCABLE'S PICK; seed f4f6f1f4.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
